# English phrasing for anime technique

Prompts read generic when every effect is called "anime style". Name the technique instead. Use one or two items per clause; stacking more than that makes the model average them into mush.

## Look and line

| 中文技法 | 英文写法 | 用在哪 |
|---|---|---|
| 赛璐璐上色 | cel-shaded flat colors | 风格定调，配合 `2D-animated` |
| 二值阴影、硬边 | two-tone shadow blocks with hard edges | 阴影字段 |
| 多层次阴影 | a three-tone shadow ramp on the face | 面部特写 |
| 均匀细线、不收锋 | thin uniform linework with no taper | 线条字段 |
| 粗细变化 | tapered line weight, thicker at the jaw and shoulders | 中景以上 |
| 有限色板、平涂 | a limited palette of flat color fills | 色调锚定 |
| 高光单点 | a single specular dot at the upper-left of the iris | 眼睛字段 |
| 逆光轮廓 | rim light along the silhouette | 黄昏、舞台灯 |
| 空气感光晕 | soft bloom across the highlights | 摄影合成 |
| 轻微色散 | slight chromatic fringing at the frame edges | 复古、老胶片感 |

## Motion and timing

| 中文技法 | 英文写法 | 用在哪 |
|---|---|---|
| 一拍二（12 张/秒） | animated on twos | 日常对话、缓慢动作 |
| 一拍一（24 张/秒） | animated on ones | 快速动作前的小段 |
| 预备动作 | a clear anticipation beat before the swing | 作用力起点 |
| 挤压与伸展 | squash and stretch through the impact | 打斗、喜剧 |
| 过冲与回弹 | the arm overshoots, then settles back | 收势、定格前 |
| 冲击帧 | a single-frame white impact flash | 击中瞬间 |
| 速度线 | hand-drawn speed lines radiating from the strike | 高速动作 |
| 拖影 | a two-frame smear trail behind the hand | 挥击 |
| 保持时长 | a held frame that lasts half a second | 定格、演出停顿 |
| 飘动 | fabric flutter that lifts once, then falls still | 衣角、发梢 |
| 中割 | in-between frames keep the motion arc unbroken | 长动作路径 |

## Staging and camera

| 中文技法 | 英文写法 | 用在哪 |
|---|---|---|
| 演出停顿 | the shot holds on the empty doorway before the cut | 情绪落点 |
| 空镜 | an establishing landscape shot with no characters | 段落开头 |
| 三分法站位 | the subject sits on the left third | 构图字段 |
| 视线引导 | the eye is led along the fence line to the figure | 构图字段 |
| 主观镜头 | the camera takes the girl's point of view | POV |
| 固定长镜 | a held static shot with no camera move | 差分幅度 0–1 级 |
| 缓慢推近 | the camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed | 情绪递进 |
| 环绕 | the camera moves in an arc around the subject | 揭示、转身 |

## Sound

| 中文技法 | 英文写法 |
|---|---|
| 环境底噪 | low room tone continues underneath |
| 单次拟音 | one wooden knock, then silence |
| 布料摩擦 | fabric sliding against the floor |
| 呼吸 | a short exhale, then a held breath |
| 音乐盒 | a single music-box line at a slow tempo |
| 低频铺底 | a sustained low string pad that slowly rises |

## Words to avoid

`cinematic`, `beautiful`, `masterpiece`, `8K`, `hyper-detailed`, `trending on artstation`, `epic`, `stunning`, `atmospheric`. None of them describe anything a camera or microphone can capture, and H3 reads them as noise.
